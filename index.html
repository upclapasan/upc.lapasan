ABOUT OUR CHURCH

Growing Together In Faith

United Pentecostal Church Philippines Inc. exists to glorify God, proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, strengthen believers, and serve our community through worship, discipleship, and Christian fellowship.

Building Faith, Strengthening Families, Serving Communities

Our church is committed to helping every believer grow spiritually through biblical teaching, heartfelt worship, prayer, and compassionate service.

✝

Christ-Centered Worship

Worship services that glorify God through praise, prayer, and His Word.

📖

Biblical Teaching

Sound biblical preaching and discipleship for every generation.

❤️

Loving Fellowship

Building meaningful relationships through unity and Christian love.

🎯

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples, baptize believers, and lead people into a personal relationship with God through the power of the Holy Spirit.

🌎

Our Vision

To become a growing church that transforms lives, strengthens families, develops future leaders, and reaches communities for Christ.
